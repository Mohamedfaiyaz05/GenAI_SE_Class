# ShopEase — Account Access & Login Policy

**Policy ID:** ACC-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-11-09

## 1. Account Registration and Sign-In

Customers can create a ShopEase account using an email address or mobile number and must verify the contact method when prompted. Existing customers can sign in with their registered credentials. A valid account is required to view saved addresses, order history, and support requests. Guest-order assistance is available through customer support after order ownership is verified.

## 2. Forgotten Password and Password Reset

Customers who forget their password should select **Forgot Password** on the sign-in page, enter their registered email address, and follow the reset link sent to that address. Password reset links expire after **30 minutes** and can be used only once. If the link expires, the customer should request a new one. Customers should check spam or junk folders if the email does not arrive within **10 minutes**. For security, support agents cannot view, disclose, or directly reset a customer's password.

## 3. One-Time Password (OTP) and Verification Issues

An OTP may be required to verify sign-in or changes to sensitive account details. OTPs expire after **5 minutes**. Customers should use only the most recent OTP and avoid repeatedly requesting new codes. If an OTP does not arrive, they should confirm network connectivity and the registered contact details, then retry after **2 minutes**. After repeated failures, support can initiate a verification review. ShopEase staff will never ask customers to share passwords, OTPs, or recovery codes.

## 4. Account Lockouts and Suspicious Sign-In Attempts

For account protection, **5 unsuccessful sign-in attempts within 15 minutes** trigger a temporary **30-minute lockout**. Customers can wait for the lockout to expire or use the password-reset process. If the account remains inaccessible after the lockout and reset steps, the issue must be referred to human support. Customers who do not recognize a sign-in alert should change their password immediately if they still have access and contact the security support team.

## 5. Updating Account Details

Customers can update their display name, contact preferences, and saved addresses from **My Account > Profile & Settings**. Changing the registered email address or phone number requires verification of the existing account and the new contact method. Changes to an address saved in the profile do **not** automatically update an order that has already been placed. Support agents must not claim to have changed account information without confirmation from an authorized system.

## 6. Compromised Accounts and Unauthorized Activity

Customers who suspect unauthorized access should stop using suspicious links, change their password if possible, and review recent account activity. Reports of unknown orders, altered contact details, unauthorized payments, or suspected identity theft require **immediate escalation to the security or payments support team**. Human support will verify ownership before restoring access or changing sensitive details. AI agents must not promise that an account has been secured, that a payment has been reversed, or that fraudulent activity has been resolved.

## 7. Account Recovery and Identity Verification

If a customer has lost access to their registered email address or phone number, self-service password reset may not be possible. Human support can begin an account-recovery review using an approved verification procedure. Customers may be asked for limited order-related information through an official secure channel. They must **never** be asked to send passwords, full payment-card numbers, CVVs, OTPs, or government identity documents through the support chat. Recovery approval and timing depend on successful verification; access cannot be guaranteed.

## 8. Account Deactivation and Data Requests

Customers can request account deactivation or deletion through **My Account > Privacy & Account Requests**, or contact human support if they cannot sign in. Requests may require identity verification. Certain transaction records may need to be retained for legal, fraud-prevention, or accounting purposes. Data access, correction, and deletion requests are handled by the privacy support team; AI agents cannot confirm deletion or specify completion dates without a verified case update.

## 9. Login Troubleshooting and Escalation

For ordinary sign-in failures, customers should confirm their email address, check for typing errors, use the password-reset option, update the app or browser, and try a private browsing session if the problem persists. Support should avoid asking for sensitive credentials or diagnosing an outage without verified service information.

Escalate when an account appears compromised; unauthorized orders or payments are reported; a customer cannot access their registered recovery method; verification repeatedly fails; an account remains locked after the documented steps; or a customer raises a legal, privacy, or identity-theft concern. A routine forgotten-password request can be **Resolved** by giving the documented self-service steps, without claiming the password was changed.
