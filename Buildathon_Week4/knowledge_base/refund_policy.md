# ShopEase — Returns, Refunds & Replacements Policy

**Policy ID:** REF-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 2026-11-09  
**Policy owner:** Customer Operations  
**Applies to:** Eligible orders placed through the ShopEase online store within India

> **Scope:** This is a fictional ShopEase policy for the customer-support demonstration. Eligibility and timeframes below are general rules. Final decisions depend on verified order information and inspection, where applicable.

## 1. Return eligibility and time window

- Most unused, eligible products may be returned **within 15 calendar days of confirmed delivery**.
- The product should be in its original condition, with accessories, manuals, tags, and packaging where reasonably available. Trying on footwear indoors to check fit does not, by itself, make it used.
- Proof of purchase is required: an order ID or receipt from ShopEase.
- Returns requested outside the 15-day window require human review; approval is not guaranteed.
- Products marked **Final Sale** or **Non-returnable** on their product page are not eligible for a change-of-mind return, but a verified defect or incorrect item can still be reviewed under applicable consumer protections.

## 2. How to request a return

1. Sign in to **ShopEase → My Orders** and select the delivered item.
2. Choose **Return / Replace**, select the reason, and provide the requested details.
3. If self-service is unavailable, contact human support with the order ID, item name, delivery date, and reason for return.
4. Follow the return instructions provided after the request is approved. Do not ship an item to an unverified address.

A return request is **not the same as an approved refund**. The order must be checked for eligibility and, where required, the returned product inspected.

## 3. Refund methods and processing timelines

- Once an eligible return is received and approved after inspection, ShopEase normally **initiates the refund within 5 business days**.
- Refunds are ordinarily issued to the **original payment method**. The bank or payment provider may take an additional **5–10 business days** to reflect the credit.
- Cash-on-delivery refunds require human support to arrange an approved refund method; customers must not share bank details in a public chat.
- Promotional discounts, vouchers, and loyalty credits may be reinstated or adjusted according to the original offer terms; the AI must not promise cash reimbursement for promotional credits.
- Original delivery fees and optional express-shipping charges are generally **non-refundable for change-of-mind returns**. Charges resulting from a confirmed ShopEase fulfillment error are reviewed by support.

## 4. Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

- Report a damaged, defective, or incorrect item **within 7 calendar days of delivery** when possible to enable prompt verification.
- Provide the order ID, a description of the issue, and photographs or a short video if safe and available. Do not operate a product that appears electrically or physically unsafe.
- For a confirmed manufacturing defect, transit damage, or incorrect item, ShopEase may offer an eligible **replacement or refund**, subject to stock and verification.
- The customer may request a replacement through **My Orders → Return / Replace**. Approval and shipment timing are not guaranteed until verified.
- A visibly broken blender reported shortly after delivery should receive these replacement-request steps. **Do not claim a replacement has already been approved or dispatched.**
- If an item is dangerous, leaking, overheating, or causing injury, advise the customer to stop using it and escalate for urgent human review.

## 5. Exchanges, partial orders, and bundles

- Size or color exchanges are available only where the product is eligible and replacement stock exists. Otherwise, an eligible return and new purchase may be necessary.
- If only one item in a multi-item order is returned, any approved refund applies to that item and its eligible adjustments, not automatically to the full order.
- Bundle discounts and buy-one-get-one promotions may be recalculated if part of the qualifying purchase is returned.
- If an order arrived in multiple shipments, the return window for each eligible item starts from **that item's confirmed delivery date**.

## 6. Items with special restrictions

- Opened personal-care and hygiene products, perishable goods, customized or personalized items, and activated digital products are generally **not returnable for change of mind** when clearly disclosed at purchase.
- Apparel and footwear may be returned within the standard window if they meet condition requirements; worn, washed, or altered products may be rejected after inspection.
- Products covered by a manufacturer warranty may require a warranty-service assessment after the standard return window.
- If a product's listing gives a different return condition, human support must verify the order-specific terms before confirming eligibility.

## 7. Cancellations and undelivered orders

- Before dispatch, customers may request cancellation through **My Orders** if the option is available; otherwise, human support must review it.
- Once dispatched, cancellation or interception is not guaranteed. The customer may need to follow the applicable delivery and return process.
- For an order that was never delivered, do not describe the case as a completed customer return. Human support must verify the delivery or courier outcome before deciding on reimbursement.
- Refunds for canceled prepaid orders, when approved, are normally initiated within **5 business days**; payment-provider posting times may vary.

## 8. Refund status, duplicate charges, and payment disputes

- Customers can review an available refund status in **My Orders** and compare it with the original payment statement.
- A refund shown as initiated may not yet be posted by the bank. Customers should allow the stated provider processing period before requesting a status investigation.
- **Duplicate or unauthorized charges, suspected fraud, chargebacks, and threats of legal action require immediate human escalation**, even if the customer also asks for a refund.
- Do not assert that a disputed charge has been reversed, promise a refund amount, or request a full card number, CVV, PIN, password, or one-time password.
- If a customer says they were charged three times, mark the case **Escalated** and request only non-sensitive transaction references through an approved support channel.

## 9. Exceptions, investigations, and escalation

Escalate to human support when:

- A return is outside the standard window or eligibility cannot be verified.
- An item is unsafe, hazardous, missing from the returned parcel, or disputed after inspection.
- The refund is overdue beyond the stated initiation or bank-posting periods and requires transaction investigation.
- There is a payment dispute, suspected fraud, legal threat, or **High-urgency / extremely angry** complaint.
- Policy evidence is insufficient, product-specific terms conflict, or the customer requests an action that requires account/payment access.

For investigation, record the order ID if provided, item, delivery date, reason, relevant evidence, payment/refund reference if safely available, and escalation reason. **AI support may explain procedures but cannot approve returns, issue payments, modify transactions, or guarantee outcomes.**
