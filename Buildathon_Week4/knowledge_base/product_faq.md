# ShopEase — Product Information, Quality & Warranty Policy

**Policy ID:** PROD-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-11-09  
**Policy Owner:** Product Support  
**Applies to:** Products sold through the ShopEase online store in India

> **Scope:** This fictional ShopEase policy describes general product-support procedures. Product-page specifications, manufacturer terms, and verified order records determine product-specific eligibility. Support must not invent specifications, stock levels, warranty coverage, or approvals.

## 1. Product Information and Specifications

- Product descriptions may include dimensions, materials, color, compatibility, care instructions, and included accessories. Customers should refer to the specific product page and model number before purchasing.
- Images are illustrative; minor appearance differences can occur because of lighting or display settings. Material differences in model, advertised features, or supplied items should be reported for review.
- Electrical products should be used only with the voltage, accessories, and operating instructions stated by the manufacturer.
- If an important specification is missing or unclear, support should request the product name or SKU and refer the question for verification rather than guess.

## 2. Availability, Variants, and Preorders

- Stock and delivery estimates shown on product pages can change before checkout. Adding an item to a cart does not reserve stock.
- Color, size, and model variants may have different prices, stock, or delivery estimates.
- Preorder release dates are estimates unless explicitly confirmed in the order record. Customers should review the product listing for applicable preorder conditions.
- AI support cannot reserve inventory, confirm real-time availability without an integration, or guarantee restock dates.

## 3. Damaged, Defective, or Nonfunctional Products

- Customers should report an item that arrives broken, leaking, unsafe, or not functioning as described as soon as practical, preferably **within 7 calendar days of delivery**, to help preserve delivery evidence. This reporting preference does not eliminate applicable statutory rights or manufacturer warranty coverage.
- To request a review, open **My Orders → Return / Replace**, choose **Damaged / Defective**, and provide the order ID, product/model, description, and clear photographs or a short video if safe to capture.
- Customers must **stop using a product that sparks, overheats, leaks hazardous contents, or presents an injury risk**. Do not ask them to operate it again for troubleshooting.
- After verification, eligible outcomes may include a replacement, repair, or refund depending on the product, stock, and applicable policy. **Approval is not automatic.**

## 4. Wrong Items, Missing Parts, and Incomplete Orders

- For an incorrect product, variant, or quantity, customers should compare the received item with the order confirmation and report the mismatch through **My Orders → Get Help**.
- For missing accessories, include the product model, packing-list details if available, and the missing component. Some accessories are sold separately and are not included unless listed on the product page.
- Orders may ship in multiple parcels. Customers should check tracking for all shipments before reporting a missing item.
- ShopEase may request photos of the package label and contents to investigate a packing error. Support must not promise a replacement part until the issue is verified.

## 5. Replacements, Exchanges, and Inspection

- A customer can request a replacement for an eligible damaged, defective, or incorrect item through **My Orders → Return / Replace**.
- The support team may arrange inspection or return pickup before approving the replacement. Customers should retain accessories and packaging where reasonably possible.
- Replacement availability depends on the same product or approved equivalent being in stock. If unavailable, human support reviews alternative remedies.
- Size or color exchanges for non-defective items are subject to product eligibility and the **15-calendar-day return window** measured from confirmed delivery; an exchange is not guaranteed.
- AI support can explain how to submit a request but cannot create a replacement order, authorize a pickup, or promise an exact delivery date without a connected order system.

## 6. Manufacturer Warranty and Repairs

- Warranty duration, covered defects, exclusions, and service locations depend on the manufacturer and product listing. **There is no universal ShopEase warranty duration for all products.**
- Manufacturing defects may be covered by a valid manufacturer warranty; accidental damage, misuse, unauthorized modifications, and ordinary wear are commonly excluded unless the specific warranty states otherwise.
- Customers should keep their invoice, model number, and serial number where applicable. Warranty claims may require manufacturer assessment or an authorized service center.
- A warranty claim is different from a standard return. Support must not guarantee free repairs or replacement without verifying the product's warranty terms.

## 7. Product Safety, Usage, and Care

- Customers should follow the manufacturer's user manual for installation, cleaning, charging, maintenance, and safe operation.
- For appliances, avoid using damaged power cords or operating a device with signs of overheating. Disconnect power if safe to do so.
- For products involving batteries, chemicals, or sharp components, do not provide improvised repair instructions. Direct the customer to manufacturer guidance or qualified service.
- If a product causes injury, smoke, fire, or an immediate safety concern, advise the customer to stop use, seek appropriate emergency assistance where necessary, and escalate the case urgently.

## 8. Product Authenticity and Listing Discrepancies

- Customers who suspect a counterfeit item, altered serial number, misleading listing, or missing certification should contact support with their order ID and relevant evidence.
- Product authenticity and certification disputes require specialist review; support must not declare an item genuine or counterfeit without verification.
- If the received product materially differs from the advertised model or features, support should document the discrepancy and arrange a review of the available remedy.
- Product reviews and third-party seller claims do not override verified ShopEase order details or manufacturer documentation.

## 9. Investigations and Human Escalation

Escalate to human support when:

- A product poses an electrical, fire, injury, chemical, or other immediate safety risk.
- The customer reports suspected counterfeiting, a serious listing discrepancy, or a disputed inspection outcome.
- A replacement requires inventory confirmation, manual authorization, warranty interpretation, or a repair assessment.
- The customer reports fraud, legal action, extreme anger, or another High-urgency issue.
- A requested product-specific fact is absent from the knowledge base and cannot be verified from an authorized source.
- The request concerns a product or service not covered by the available catalog or policies, such as **custom-branded corporate gift cards**, and human confirmation is required.

For investigations, record the order ID if available, product name/SKU, model or serial number where relevant, delivery date, issue description, and any safely supplied evidence. Never request payment credentials, passwords, or OTPs. **AI support may provide policy-based guidance but must not claim to have inspected an item, confirmed inventory, approved a replacement, or contacted the manufacturer unless a connected system confirms that action.**
