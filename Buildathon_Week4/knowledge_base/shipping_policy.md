# ShopEase — Shipping, Delivery & Tracking Policy

**Policy ID:** SHIP-001  
**Version:** 1.2  
**Effective date:** 2026-11-09  
**Policy owner:** Customer Operations  
**Applies to:** Orders placed through the ShopEase online store within India

> **Scope:** This is a fictional policy for the ShopEase customer-support demonstration. The AI assistant may explain published rules but cannot access live orders, initiate investigations, issue refunds, or promise delivery dates.

## 1. Order processing and delivery estimates

- Orders are normally processed within **1–2 business days** after payment confirmation, excluding public holidays.
- **Standard delivery:** typically **3–7 business days after dispatch**.
- **Express delivery:** typically **1–3 business days after dispatch**, where available.
- The estimated delivery date shown at checkout or in **My Orders** is an estimate, not a guaranteed delivery commitment.
- Business days exclude Sundays and applicable courier/public holidays. Remote delivery areas, peak-sale periods, severe weather, and transport disruptions may extend timelines.
- An order marked **Processing** has not necessarily been handed to the courier; **Shipped/In Transit** means dispatch has occurred.

## 2. Tracking an order

1. Sign in to **ShopEase → My Orders**.
2. Open the relevant order and select **Track Shipment**.
3. Check the latest courier scan and estimated delivery date. The dispatch email may also contain a tracking link.

Tracking scans can take **up to 24 hours after dispatch** to appear. If no tracking update appears for **more than 48 hours after dispatch**, the customer may contact support for a tracking review. The AI must not claim to know the package's current location without an authorized tracking integration.

## 3. Delayed delivery

- If the estimated delivery date passed **1–3 business days ago**, advise the customer to review tracking and allow for a short courier delay. This alone does not require human escalation.
- If the package is **more than 3 business days past the estimated delivery date**, a human support representative must investigate with the courier. Mark the case **Escalated**.
- If tracking shows **no movement for 5 business days**, escalate for a delivery investigation even if the estimated date has not yet passed.
- If the customer needs the item for a time-sensitive event, acknowledge the concern but do not guarantee delivery or compensation. Escalate if there is a high-urgency situation or a request for an action the AI cannot perform.
- Delivery delay does not automatically entitle the customer to a refund or replacement. Any refund request must be assessed under the separate refund policy.

## 4. Delivered but not received / missing package

If tracking says **Delivered** but the customer cannot find the parcel:

1. Ask them to check the doorstep, reception/security desk, safe-drop location, and household members, where appropriate.
2. Ask them to confirm the delivery address shown in their order confirmation, without sharing sensitive details publicly.
3. If still missing, **escalate immediately** for proof-of-delivery review. Do not assert that the courier or customer is at fault.

Suspected theft, forged delivery confirmation, or delivery fraud must be escalated. The AI must not promise a replacement or refund before investigation.

## 5. Address changes, cancellations, and failed delivery attempts

- **Before dispatch:** Customers may request an address correction or cancellation through human support; approval depends on order status.
- **After dispatch:** Address changes and shipment interception are not guaranteed and require human support/courier confirmation.
- **Failed delivery attempts:** Customers should follow the courier's official reattempt instructions shown in tracking. Reattempt schedules vary by courier and location.
- **Returned to sender:** If an undelivered parcel is being returned to the warehouse, human support must confirm next steps. The AI must not promise automatic reshipment or reimbursement.

## 6. Damaged parcels, wrong items, and partial deliveries

- For a **visibly damaged outer parcel**, advise the customer to photograph the packaging and item before discarding materials, where safe.
- For **wrong, damaged, or missing items**, ask the customer to retain the order details and photographs, then refer to the **product FAQ** and **refund policy** for eligibility and timelines.
- For **split shipments**, different items in one order may have different tracking numbers and delivery dates. Check each shipment in **My Orders**.
- Escalate cases involving unsafe or hazardous damage, suspected tampering, or disputed delivery evidence.

## 7. Shipping charges and service availability

- Shipping fees and express-delivery eligibility are displayed at checkout and may vary by location, basket value, and promotion.
- The AI must not invent a free-shipping threshold, express availability, or shipping-fee refund rule when the order details or applicable promotion are unavailable.
- International shipping is **not covered by this policy**; such inquiries must be referred to human support for confirmation.

## 8. Mandatory escalation triggers

Mark the ticket **Escalated** when any of these applies:

- Delivery is **more than 3 business days late**.
- Tracking has not moved for **5 business days**.
- Package is marked delivered but is missing.
- Suspected fraud, theft, tampering, legal threats, or payment disputes.
- Customer is extremely angry or the case is classified **High urgency**.
- Relevant policy evidence is missing or the requested action requires order/courier access.

**Escalation record:** Capture the order ID if supplied, issue summary, last known tracking status if supplied, relevant dates, source document, and reason for escalation. Never request payment-card numbers, passwords, or one-time passwords.

## 9. Delivery investigation and customer updates

- A delivery investigation requires the **order ID**, the **estimated delivery date**, the **last tracking status**, and a concise description of the issue, when available.
- Human support may review courier scan history, delivery attempts, proof of delivery, and any customer-provided photographs or messages.
- An investigation does **not** guarantee a refund, replacement, or a revised delivery date. The outcome depends on verified shipment details and applicable order policies.
- Customers should retain their order confirmation and any courier notifications until the case is closed.
- If a courier or support representative supplies an investigation reference, customers can include it in later follow-ups to avoid repeating details.
- If the customer follows up while an investigation is pending, the AI may explain the process but must **not** invent an investigation status, outcome, or completion deadline.
